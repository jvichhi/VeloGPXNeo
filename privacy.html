🚴 VeloGPX

Privacy Policy — Last updated: May 9, 2026

VeloGPX (“the App”) is developed and maintained by J Vichhi (“we”, “our”, or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

VeloGPX is designed to work entirely on your device. We collect as little data as possible:

  • Location data — Your GPS position is used in real time while you ride to display your position on the route map, detect off-route conditions, and calculate proximity to points of interest. Location data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Route and GPX files — Files you import (GPX, GeoJSON) are stored locally in the app's sandboxed storage. We do not upload or access these files remotely.
  • Health & fitness data — If HealthKit integration is enabled, ride metrics (distance, duration) may be written to Apple Health. This data remains under your control inside the Apple Health ecosystem.
  • Apple Watch sync — Ride state (speed, distance, next POI) is sent to your paired Apple Watch via WatchConnectivity. This transfer stays local over your device's Bluetooth/Wi-Fi connection and does not leave your devices.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers.
  • We do not create user accounts or require registration.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services that transmit data off-device.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetise any user data.

3. Third-Party Services

VeloGPX uses Apple’s MapKit framework to render maps. Map tile requests are handled by Apple, subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. No other third-party SDKs that collect user data are included in the App.

4. Data Storage & Security

All app data — including imported routes and stored POIs — is saved in the app’s sandboxed local storage on your iPhone. Data may be included in iCloud or iTunes backups if you have those features enabled on your device; this is governed by Apple’s terms and your own iCloud settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

VeloGPX is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact us at the email address associated with the App Store listing.
