VeloGPX

Privacy Policy — Last updated: May 9, 2026

VeloGPX is built and maintained by J Vichhi. This page covers what data the app touches and what happens to it.

What the app uses

  • Location — Your GPS position is read while you ride to show your position on the map, flag off-route moments, and calculate distance to upcoming POIs. It never leaves your device.
  • Route files — GPX and GeoJSON files you import are saved in the app’s local storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Health data — If you allow HealthKit access, ride distance and duration are written to Apple Health. That data lives in your Health app and is governed by Apple’s own privacy terms.
  • Apple Watch — Speed, distance, and the next POI are sent to your Watch over WatchConnectivity (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi between your own devices). Nothing goes to a server.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no user profile.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that phone home.
  • No advertising. No data sold or shared with third parties.
  • No names, emails, or identifiers collected — ever.

Third-party frameworks

Maps are rendered using Apple’s MapKit. Tile requests go to Apple and fall under Apple’s Privacy Policy. No other external SDKs are included.

Backups

App data is stored in the iOS sandbox and may be included in your iCloud or local iPhone backup if you have backups enabled. That’s controlled entirely by your device settings.

Kids

VeloGPX is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Updates

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too. Nothing will change quietly.

Questions

Reach out via GitHub Issues or through the email on the App Store listing.
